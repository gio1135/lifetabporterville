OUR WATER

Sourced from protected natural springs and artesian aquifers in Italy, DISANTINO is shaped by a rare geological composition and elevated bicarbonate content. This distinctive mineral balance creates a smooth, refined mouthfeel that complements fine dining while remaining gentle for everyday enjoyment.

DISANTINO’s bicarbonate level sits at the ideal balance for fine dining. It is sufficiently elevated to support digestion and gently counterbalance the acidity of wines and rich sauces, yet precisely calibrated to preserve a clean, smooth mouthfeel—never salty, never chalky.
